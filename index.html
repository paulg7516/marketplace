Quick Reminder

A Chrome extension for the things that cross your mind between tasks. Type a note the way you'd say it, "follow up with Sarah friday 2pm", and get a desktop notification when it's time. Nothing leaves your machine.

Chrome extension. Download

How to install
  1. Unzip the download somewhere it won't get cleaned up.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the quick-reminder folder.
  4. Pin it, then press ⌘ Shift E (Ctrl Shift E on Windows).
