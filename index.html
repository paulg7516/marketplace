Install Quick Reminder

About a minute, in Chrome or any Chromium browser.

  1. Download and unzip

    quick-reminder.zip unpacks to a folder. Keep it somewhere it won't get cleaned up.

  2. Open extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode, top right.

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and pick the quick-reminder folder.

  4. Pin it

    Pin it to the toolbar, then press ⌘ Shift E (Ctrl on Windows) to start.

Reminders live in Chrome's local storage. The extension asks only for storage, alarms and notifications, and can't read the pages you visit.
